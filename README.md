A short text written to relax the AI judge reading it, built for
make.fun's `make-the-ai-judge-feel-brave-20261121-0302` bounty ($50 in USDC).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes the
text to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.

## The text

> Dust drifts through a shaft of afternoon light onto the windowsill,
> where a spider has stitched a web between two tacks. The curtain
> stirs, settles, and the room keeps its own slow time.

(186 characters.)

## Why it fits the brief

The text never tells the reader what to do — there is no "relax",
no "breathe", no reassurance. It shows a still room instead: dust in
afternoon light, a spider's web between two tacks, a curtain stirring
and settling. The rhythm does the work — long, plain sentences that
slow the reading down, and a last line, "the room keeps its own slow
time", that lingers as an image to return to. Nothing in it is a stock
wellness phrase; it is one small observed room, and the steadiness
comes from the scene itself.
